# Livestock Ration Formulator

A practical decision-support project for evaluating livestock rations, nutrient supply, feed cost and production-oriented feeding strategies.

## Project Purpose

Livestock rations should be evaluated not only by ingredient quantity, but also by their contribution to:

- Dry matter intake
- Energy and crude protein
- Fibre adequacy
- Feed cost
- Cost per animal per day
- Production potential
- Feed efficiency and profitability

This project aims to connect animal-nutrition science with simple analytical tools that can support farm-level and management decisions.

## Initial Scope

The first version will support:

- Ingredient-wise ration entry
- As-fed and dry-matter calculations
- Crude-protein supply
- Energy supply
- NDF and ADF evaluation
- Daily feed cost
- Comparison with target nutrient requirements
- Identification of nutrient deficits and excesses
- Summary recommendations

## Planned Development

### Phase 1 — Basic Ration Evaluation

- Dairy cattle ration calculator
- Feed-cost calculator
- Nutrient-supply summary
- Sample feed-ingredient dataset

### Phase 2 — Production-Based Analysis

- Maintenance and milk-production requirements
- Ration comparison by production group
- Feed cost per litre of milk
- Income over feed cost
- Scenario and sensitivity analysis

### Phase 3 — Expanded Livestock Applications

- Beef feedlot ration and cost-of-gain analysis
- Buffalo feeding models
- Calf and replacement-heifer models
- Farm-performance dashboards
- Excel and Power BI integration

## Intended Users

- Animal nutritionists
- Dairy and beef consultants
- Livestock farmers and farm managers
- Veterinary and animal-science students
- Researchers and extension professionals
- Agribusiness and livestock-project analysts

## Scientific and Practical Note

Outputs from this project are intended for education, preliminary assessment and decision support. Final feeding recommendations should consider animal type, breed, body weight, physiological stage, production level, feed analysis, environment, health and farm-management conditions.

The calculator should not replace laboratory feed testing or professional ration formulation.

## Data Privacy

Only demonstration or anonymized datasets should be uploaded. Client identities, farm records, commercial formulations, unpublished research and confidential government or business information must not be published.
